      About Boardu

      Notes that hang on a board

      Every card hangs from a pin and sways in a gentle wind. Drag one and let go to watch it swing. Notes understand Markdown: headings, bold, italics, code, links and tick-box lists. Each note can take one of nine colours, six fonts, five tilts and its own text size, line height and letter spacing. Mini notes are small slips for a name, a link or a number.

      Fourteen mini apps

      • Pomodoro timer, countdown and reminders, all with a soft chime.
      • Task checklist, all habits, monthly habit, mood journal, water and weight trackers.
      • Calendar and a clock with five faces.
      • Pictures, saved links and YouTube videos that play on the board.

      Eighteen boards to pin them to

      Candy, Light, Dark and Midnight. Cork with push pins, a workshop wall of real-looking planks with nailed Post-its, a chalkboard with magnets, a notebook with washi tape, a sketchbook in ink, and a blueprint. On the screen side: a terminal where notes become windows, and a hologram where they are projected in light. Every surface is painted by your browser, so wood has grain and knots, cork has granules and paper has tooth.

      Festive boards

      Diwali with marigolds and lamplight, Halloween under the moon, Christmas with baubles and fairy lights, New Year with gold stars, Holi with bursts of colour, and Eid with lanterns. Each has its own gentle snow, sparks, fog or confetti, which you can turn off in Settings. When a festival is close, Boardu offers its board once.

      Keeping things in order

      • Several boards, search across all of them, and jump straight to a match.
      • Select many cards with a lasso or Shift-click, then move, recolour, archive or delete them together.
      • Snap to grid, Tidy up, undo and redo, an archive, and a trash that keeps cards for 30 days. Drop a card on the bin, or flick it off the screen and confirm.
      • Give any board its own theme, and the others keep theirs.

      Your data, your files

      • Back up to a file and restore it on another computer. Restoring merges, and the newer copy of a note wins.
      • In Chrome and Edge, keep a synced backup file in OneDrive or on a USB stick. Boardu updates it as you work, and on another computer you open it to carry on.
      • Save a board as a picture, or your notes as Markdown.
      • Works offline once it has loaded, and can be installed like an app.

      A pin board that lives in your browser

      Boardu is a free place to pin notes, lists and little tools. There is no sign-up and no account. What you write stays in this browser, on this device.

      Designed and built by shreyas ↗.

      Made with

      Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single page. Icons are adapted from Lucide (ISC licence). Typefaces are Figtree, Nunito, Kalam, Caveat, Literata and JetBrains Mono, served by Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font Licence. Saving a board as PNG uses html-to-image (MIT licence).

      Version

      Your notes stay on your device

      Everything you put on a board is saved in this browser's own storage on this computer. Boardu has no server that receives your notes, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

      When Boardu talks to the internet

      • Fonts. The typefaces load from Google Fonts. Like any site that uses them, this sends your IP address and browser details to Google.
      • Pictures and links you add. A picture added by link loads from the site that hosts it. A saved link asks that site for its small icon.
      • YouTube. A YouTube card shows a thumbnail from YouTube's image server. The video loads from youtube-nocookie.com only when you press play.
      • Saving as PNG. The first time you do this, Boardu downloads a small open-source helper from jsDelivr.

      Backups and exports

      Backups, pictures and Markdown files are saved to your own downloads folder. Boardu never uploads them anywhere.

      Synced backup file

      If you turn on Keep a synced backup file (Chrome and Edge), Boardu writes your boards to the one file you choose, and nowhere else. If that file is in a cloud folder such as OneDrive, your cloud service copies it the way it copies any of your files. Boardu itself still sends nothing over the internet.

      This browser remembers which file you chose. On a later visit it may ask you to reconnect the file before Boardu writes to it again. Stop syncing or Clear all data makes it forget the file. The file itself stays where it is. On a shared computer, do one of these before you leave.

      Notifications

      Timers and reminders can show a notification only if you allow it. Your browser shows those notifications, and you can turn them off in its settings at any time.

      Keeping your data safe

      Clearing this browser's data, or a shared computer that wipes its profile, deletes your boards. Use Settings, Back up to file before that happens, or keep a synced backup file.
